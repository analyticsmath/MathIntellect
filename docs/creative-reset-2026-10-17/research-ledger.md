## Diagnosis and new story

The rejected build repeated left-copy/right-graph compositions, exposed internal scene labels as eyebrows, used a very narrow image cast, and measured success with engineering checks. Its route animation depended on a trajectory actor that only some links carried. Its footer did not meet the requested scale.

New story: curiosity → a changed assumption → an appropriate model → an inspectable result → another question. Photography is editorial metaphor, never evidence that the product controls aircraft, predicts physical systems or is endorsed by a photographed organization. The new public story contains no decorative charts. The public example retains the genuine deterministic calculation but presents its numeric results directly. Account model descriptions are based on the product truth audit, not old illustrative engines.
