# 0. A DIRECTOR'S NOTE

We are not redesigning a landing page. We are directing a work of interactive cinema whose subject is **how one chosen condition changes a possible outcome**. The audience has to *feel* that choice, see its downstream consequences, and then be able to touch the actual mechanism.

The previous eight-act draft was a logical content outline, not yet cinematic enough: a succession of explanations punctuated by photographs and charts. This film must instead behave like a coherent dramatic scene. It has a protagonist, a tension, a reveal, a human intervention, a consequence, a pause, a wider world and a return.

An image cannot be selected merely because it illustrates the word "uncertainty." Each image is chosen for its role in the audience's emotional understanding of this exact moment. No ten-heading inventory and no compulsory numbered chapter labels are visible to visitors.

**Principal dramatic question:** What changes when you change just one thing?

**Product proof:** A seeded browser-side Monte Carlo demonstration can retain the seed and other parameters, vary volatility, and recompute results. The full authenticated backend supports wider model categories and replay/snapshot routes. We do not imply that the physical photographs are generated by, or mathematically simulated by, Math Intellect.

**Protagonist:** A SINGLE EXPERIMENTAL SCENARIO, whose parameters and seed are never lost between scenes. This is product-native and persistent. We give it an *identifiable, tangible photographic opening* — a photographed physical event suspended between cause and effect — but its continuity is numerical and semantic, not dependent on keeping one arbitrary picture element moving forever.

**Visual motif:** THE TRACE. One true trajectory derived from the seeded scenario. It is thin, actual, anchored, then becomes branching paths, a control, paired evidence and a replay. The trace is NEVER a meaningless decorative line. One physical photograph can be reprised with changed crop/composition; the real data supplies its second life.

**Archetypal opening shot:** authentic high-speed/macro photography of a physical event at the instant before its effect becomes visible (e.g., a suspended water drop/refraction experiment), preferably a coherent *series* by one photographer. This is the art-direction search target, NOT a claim that the site currently owns a matching licensed image. If photography cannot be sourced coherently, the hero subject must be re-selected before coding, not filled with a stock road/river/rail image.

**One line of brand promise:** "Change one condition. See what follows."

# 2. THE FILM: FIVE CHAPTERS / FOURTEEN SHOTS

**Audience mode:** a continuous editorial film, interrupted twice by honest controls. The film is scroll-driven and meaningfully reversible. Navigation remains ordinary semantic links. Each chapter is a narrative *state*, not an HTML card group. No visitor-facing labels "Act 01", "Movement 05", "1 / 4", "ENG 02" or dummy storyboard notation.

Intensity curve (0–5, where 5 is spectacle):
PROLOGUE 4 → OBSERVE 2 → DEFINE 3 → MULTIPLY 5 → HOLD 1 → INTERVENE 4 → COMPARE 4 → QUIET 1 → ATLAS 5 → REPLAY 2 → FINALE 4 → UTILITY 1.

## CHAPTER I — THE MOMENT BEFORE

### SHOT 01 / Cold open — audience tension
**Still:** a real photographic event at the threshold of transformation, full bleed or 85% dominant. Extreme clarity, visible tactile detail, generous darkness/negative space, beautifully controlled crop. The interface is quiet.
**Copy appearing in the image:** "What if one thing changed?"
**Supporting line only when useful:** "Explore the conditions behind an outcome."
**Motion:** no fake opening video; the still is immediate. A modest camera push/inner-image scale evolves with first scroll. Type is initially legible, not hidden for a 5–10 second preloader.
**Staging:** header and CTA remain usable at first paint. One hairline witness mark corresponds to the real demo's baseline setting when that state becomes visible.
**Next-state anticipation:** at the last 10% of first scene, a sharp image detail or measured frame appears from beneath.
**Mobile:** portrait art direction, not the desktop crop squeezed. Headline remains readable without overlaying the photographed subject.

### SHOT 02 / Freeze-frame — the question becomes inspectable
**Still:** whole photograph hangs in space; a restrained crop fractures out of the source, sized like a photographic negative in an archive.
**Copy:** "An outcome is only part of the story."
**Motion:** full-bleed frame compresses to asymmetric, unusually proportioned evidence plate. The photo retains the *same* real subject and focal point; a duplicate crop can diverge at a different speed.
**Transition cause:** the user is visually inspecting the previously unexamined situation, NOT launching a decorative animation.

### SHOT 03 / Extract — isolate the variable
**Still:** photograph now occupies 35–45% of width; the remaining space is spare and precise. A real editable input, σ=0.35, enters alongside the source. Seed=42 is disclosed briefly but never dressed as a fake product identifier.
**Copy:** "Every result rests on an assumption."
**Motion:** photo/trace registration connects the physical editorial setup to the actual model variable. The first mathematical mark is real and restrained.
**Exit:** the calibration frame becomes the opening anchor of the model simulation in the next chapter.

## CHAPTER II — THE POSSIBLE WORLDS

### SHOT 04 / Initiate — a choice creates a field
**Still:** one seed-bound simulation trajectory takes possession of most of the page. Its origin remains fixed; related paths fan out from the same coordinate.
**Copy:** "One starting point. Many possible outcomes."
**Motion:** the real 24-path deterministic Monte Carlo result is rendered in staggered, controlled SVG trajectory reveals (a rendering choice, not fabricated paths). Photo remains as a peripheral cropped memory rather than disappearing into a wholly unrelated scene.
**Ownership:** initial data originates from runDeterministicMonteCarlo(0.35, 0.05, 50, 24, 42).
**Accessible text:** provide a short description of what the plot means, accessible via normal DOM and not dependent on animation.

### SHOT 05 / Cross the field — perspective changes
**Still:** a selection of the path field occupies a wide cinematic view. The camera sweeps laterally over cropped photographic material as actual trajectory geometry builds on top.
**Motion:** one horizontal camera-like displacement with near/medium/far layers — NOT a fake horizontal wheel hijack with lost vertical progress. Short containment, natural wheel-to-progress conversion, stable return.
**Purpose:** communicate that the sample has many outcomes but one consistent experimental setup.
**Hold:** as all 24 paths resolve, the stage settles into a genuine reading state.

### SHOT 06 / Silence — an earned pause
**Still:** almost nothing moves. One clear number and label, real sample variance, is legible with its units/context. No five-metric strip. 
**Copy:** "The spread is measurable."
**Handoff:** tiny motion within the line field alerts the viewer to the one parameter about to change.
**Why:** contrast prevents the site from becoming a motion demo and gives cognitive space for what follows.

## CHAPTER III — THE INTERRUPTION

### SHOT 07 / Intervention — touch the condition
**Still:** two visual realities share a calibrated origin. One accessible native range control (volatility) sits at the exact region of the change.
**Copy:** "Change the condition."
**Interaction:** a real σ range input adjusts the model's current sigma while keeping μ, seed, steps and path count constant. Never override active user control with scroll-driven updates. Preserve entered value through nearby scenes and route navigation where useful. Use an explicit accessible label and visible current value.
**Motion:** old paths briefly remain as quiet evidence, new computed paths move into the frame. The difference originates from data, not from a generic shape morph.
**Boundary:** this is a public demonstrator; do not advertise it as backend market/strategy execution.

### SHOT 08 / Collision — compare two possible worlds
**Still:** full-viewport matched split view with *the same axis*. Baseline and changed outcomes are legible together.
**Copy:** "Now see what moved."
**Motion:** a left-to-right take-over cut traces from shared origin → divergence → numerical output. Tiny metric detail (variance delta) is computed from actual baseline/current results. Difference semantics have a color reserved solely for changed state.
**Design law:** do not imply a deterministic mathematical guarantee that changing sigma necessarily raises realized sample variance for every sample; report the actual sampled result.
**Hold:** retain both scenarios after scroll settles; hover/focus selects the active scenario without hiding baseline.

### SHOT 09 / Resolution of evidence — knowledge, not hype
**Still:** two trajectories stop. A quiet single sentence remains over a strong crop of original photograph.
**Copy:** "The difference is in the conditions."
**Real proof:** one compact comparison row, current vs baseline, from the same underlying calculation.
**Handoff:** numeric expression recedes into a new photographic index, opening a broader question: what else can we model?

## CHAPTER IV — OTHER QUESTIONS, OTHER MODELS

### SHOT 10 / The horizontal model cinema — signature gallery
**The site-wide signature horizontal set piece.**
**Still:** five distinctly art-directed *photographic chapters*, each with its own shape and subject, but all composed with one strong visual system. One dominant frame (~55–70vw); the next frame's edge is already visible (~5–12%) to advertise further travel; secondary crop overlaps during handoff.
**The five actual lenses:** Monte Carlo (variation), Game Theory (strategic choices), Market (shocks/paths), Conflict (repeated interaction), Custom (define a variable). Photographic choices must be specific enough to support the concept; do not put a river photo next to the label "Market" simply because rivers flow.
**User-facing headlines:** 
- "When chance enters the equation." — Monte Carlo.
- "When every choice changes another." — Game Theory.
- "When the conditions shift." — Market.
- "When actions echo." — Conflict.
- "When the question is yours." — Custom.
**Mechanics:** One desktop horizontal progress mapping from vertical scroll through an overflow-visible/hidden hybrid photographic track; the stage is pinned only for the actual useful width and reading dwell. Each panel has different crop, width, foreground speed and hover/focus image takeover. Earlier media is residue in the next. On the final panel the track compresses into a selected frame — not a sudden disappearance.
**Exact choreography for a panel handoff:** READ → ANTICIPATE 5–12% next image → TRAVEL outer frame laterally while inner image counter-parallaxes → HOLD → OVERLAP next image at 20–40% visual ownership → TAKEOVER → RESOLVE → READ. Position/velocity are calculated from real element widths; do not hard-code huge +5000% pinned distance.
**Controls:** keyboard-accessible engine links /models#monte-carlo etc; standard pointer hover and focus previews. No sideways-scroll-only trap. Tabs/arrows where necessary. Non-animated and mobile version uses vertical immersive image chapters or accessible native horizontal swipe with clear arrows; a text list is available without JS.
**Not a copy of Studio Arde/Fabian:** we reproduce the *interaction mechanism* and deliberate rhythm, not their specific layout/imagery/art.

### SHOT 11 / Choice — select a mathematical lens
**Still:** selected photographic frame becomes larger, while the engine title settles close to the selected subject.
**Motion:** exact selected element rectangle is captured *before* navigation. For /models, transition to the *real* matching destination element after mount, with preloaded destination image and proper ownership handoff.
**Copy:** "Different questions need different mathematics."
**Purpose:** the horizontal moment becomes a useful route-selection instrument, not a decorative gallery.

## CHAPTER V — THE RETURN

### SHOT 12 / Replay — back to the same beginning
**Still:** protagonist returns: same seed, same starting position, same original question. The photograph revisits its opening crop with new emotional context.
**Copy:** "A result worth returning to."
**Real proof:** replay the browser-side demonstration with the same seed and parameters and assert numerically equivalent output; on the Method route explain authenticated replay conditions and limitations. Avoid false verification badges.
**Motion:** one trajectory retracts/recreates cleanly rather than an unrelated animated chart.

### SHOT 13 / The release — an invitation with conviction
**Still:** full photographic wide frame or convincing close-up reprise; no central bordered CTA card. Distinct typographic lockup.
**Copy:** "Make the unknown something you can work with."
**Action:** "Try the workbench" → /workbench; "Start modeling" → /signup.
**Timing:** primary action is usable as soon as scene enters; avoid 5 seconds of mandatory 'outro'.
**Motion:** protagonist line smoothly resolves into a large horizontal typographic baseline.

### SHOT 14 / The finale — monumental identity
**Still:** bottom of the scene becomes an authored ending with a huge MATH INTELLECT wordmark. Selected real photograph may emerge behind/crop through type, not a generic landscape unrelated to the product. Utility links then enter with high contrast.
**Visual law:** large closing brand name; every route has a substantial version. Mobile must recompose wordmark into two clear lines, not scale to illegibility.
**Motion:** subtle sticky-footer rise from the previous image and one final photographic crop; the narrative ends BEFORE site-wide links/copyright.
**Fallback:** full textual footer always exists, without WebGL or motion.

# 3. EDITING GRAMMAR: THE HANDOFF IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN THE EFFECT

Each critical scene transition must answer:
1. What stays? (seed, active parameter, photograph focal object, trace origin)
2. What changes? (crop, frame, numerical model state, selected lens)
3. What arrives before the old state exits? (anticipation)
4. Where can the eye rest? (readability hold)
5. What survives as residue? (a real prior path, physical texture or numerical label)
6. What activates the next state? (scroll, explicit click/drag, route)
7. Can reverse scroll rebuild it exactly? (no fixed-time replay upon backscroll)
8. What happens on mobile/reduced-motion? (authored replacement, not invisible screen)

Primary site film has two intense peaks, not unbroken maximum movement:
- Peak A: physical source → mathematical paths, around Shot 04–05.
- Peak B: changed condition → comparison, Shot 07–08.
Signature broadening moment: horizontal models gallery, Shot 10.
Quiet relief: Shot 06 and Shot 09/12.
Final emotional return: Shots 13–14.

**Scroll authority:** Lenis, desktop/fine-pointer only; GSAP ScrollTrigger macro timelines from actual scroll progress; native scroll on mobile/touch or prefers-reduced-motion; DOM and SVG are the readable fallback. Do not add unnecessary Three.js for visual sophistication. Shader upgrade ONLY if it transforms real media and can fail without hiding HTML.

**Never animate one transform with two owners.** No nested scroll bars. No mouse-only interactions. No decorative spinner loop unless it's genuinely a tactile model index state; a spinner image from the design folder is inspiration, not a feature requirement.

# 4. ROUTE AS A FILM GENRE

## / — CINEMATIC
One continuous causal experiment; the main signature is its source-to-outcome transformation, with one actual horizontal photographic field as a deliberate wide-screen passage.

## /models — EXPLORATORY ATLAS
**Opening:** arrival from Shot 11's selected image. 
**Composition:** a wall-sized current model image contrasted with short, useful model explanation; selected model physically makes room for its equation, required inputs, what the engine returns, and boundaries. Different engines have distinct spatial silhouettes; stop repeating equal cards/rows.
**Interaction:** selected engine can move through a tangible gallery mechanism; focus and keyboard arrows retain access; anchors remain semantic. 
**Continuity:** same trace/parameter motif changes mathematical role across engine chapters.
**Conversion:** context-specific "Try this model" leads to signup or real supported public demo; do not imply all engines are available in the public simplified Workbench.
**Mobile:** stacked photographic atlas with engine links and meaningful equations in flow.

## /workbench — INTERACTIVE / INSTRUMENT
**Opening:** Home's model field becomes a working instrument, without losing seed/parameter context.
**Physical stage:** one dominant actual-path viewport (not a grid of many charts); the controls are integrated but plainly accessible.
**User action:** changing sigma recomputes real baseline/current results; no fake telemetry. Play, reset, compare and replay must perform what they say. Keep public disclaimer that backend engine is more capable.
**Behavior:** once visitor interacts, user state owns the scene; scroll never tramples a chosen value.
**Micro-motion:** short state interpolation justified by numerical changes. On this route utility and reliability outweigh spectacle.
**Exit:** real CTA to authenticated app.

## /method — SCIENTIFIC PUBLICATION
**Opening:** cinematic subtraction. The trace from Workbench becomes a baseline rule in a restrained reading spread.
**Pacing:** intentional quiet typography, real equations, honest reproducibility disclosures, complete chapter navigation; no gratuitous huge animation.
**One editorial horizontal journey:** only where genuinely useful — a four-part *Reproducibility Ledger* (input snapshot → seed → result → replay) that shows a real relationship, with 4 large readable images/diagrams, not a decorative carousel. On mobile recompose into vertical annotated sequence. Never use as an obstruction for readers.
**Trust:** differentiate mathematical models, AI interpretation and their constraints. Existing verified explanations preserved, factual errors corrected only after source review.

Default design target is <600ms for public-to-public transition under normal readiness; avoid artificial wait. Measure durations only after browser QA.

# 6. PHOTOGRAPHY IS CASTING

Cast each photo like a performer, not fill.
- CAST A / The Cause: same identifiable physical process across hero, crop and reprise; one master source + one supporting angle ideally from same licensed series.
- CAST B / The Experiment: hands/instrument or real controlled intervention with enough negative space for the single σ input. Use only if visually coherent with Cast A.
- CAST C / The Other Systems: specific, unambiguous environment for each model category: strategic human choices, genuine market infrastructure, collective interaction, or precisely measured physical systems; no arbitrary travel photograph.
- CAST D / The Evidence: same subject/matched crop from earlier shot; avoid a new unrelated aerial.
- CAST E / Closing: the opening subject returns altered by framing alone, not by AI-generated or fake physics.

**Source condition:** use authorized licensed photography; do not manufacture or invent images. Existing repo has photographer-credit-bearing Unsplash imagery and locally optimized AVIF/WebP derivatives, but the current crosswalk/ports/rail photoworld was judged irrelevant by the user. Replace selected roles only after provenance/licensing and focal crops are verified. Unsplash Plus assets require an authenticated user entitlement/download; no connected Unsplash+ tool is currently verified. Public Unsplash Free images may be used under their applicable license with attribution/provenance retained. We must be explicit about which source each media item uses.

Photo acceptance: visual quality 8+ still, actual subject relevance, 1440×900 and mobile portrait cropping, no visible stock watermark, enough source resolution for high-density displays, compression QA without mushy motion, alt text and license record, no uncited client work. Do not load huge originals on mobile; prefetch only next critical hero/destination media.
